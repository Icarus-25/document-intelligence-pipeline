## Known Limitations

- Scanned PDFs sometimes do not read well because the text is part of the picture, not real text. Turning on OCR can help, but it can also make the program slower.
- Very complex page layouts can have slightly messy reading order.
- Images in documents are only described — they are not saved as separate image files.

Overall, most normal PDFs and Word documents come out pretty clean.
